# 2026-11-16

## MCP: list_fonts (from `experimental`)

- New read-only tool `list_fonts` (optional `filter`, case-insensitive part of the name): returns `count` and `fonts`, the installed families `emboss_text` accepts in `font`. It runs without closing the user's open tool.
- `emboss_text` with a font that is not installed now answers with up to five similar families (containing the name or sharing a word of three letters or more) and points to `list_fonts`. Logic in `mcp::list_fonts` / `mcp::find_font`, tested in `tests/mcp.rs`.

## MCP: files sent inline

- An agent in a sandbox or on another machine could not import the SVG it wrote: every file argument was a path the app read on its own machine. `open`, `import`, `load_image`, `emboss_text` and `apply_texture` now also take `file_name` + `file_text` or `file_base64` (`mcp::with_file` adds them to the schemas; `path`/`image` are no longer required).
- `mcp::inline_file` writes the file to `<temp>/buildcraft3d-agent-files/<pid>/<n>/<name>` and hands the tool its path, so the handlers did not change. Only the base name is kept (no `..` or folders), a new numbered folder per file (an emboss picture may still be in use), 25 MB at most (`INLINE_MAX`). `mcp::inline_dir` removes folders over a day old the first time it runs. Base64 decoding is a small local function (standard or URL-safe alphabet).
- Tested in `tests/mcp.rs` and end to end over HTTP against a running app: an SVG sent as text and as base64 imported as two objects per drawing.

## Smooth works as the original's

- From the original's binary and new captures (a cube and a cylinder, Smoothness at half and full, Smooth edges off and on, wireframe): the Smoothness slider binds `SmoothValue`/`SubdivisionValue` and only changes how fine the surface is (half and full have the same shape); Smooth edges binds `UseSubdivision`. Off, the cube becomes a pillow through its original corners (same size); on, a smaller round blob.
- `solid::smooth_by` now subdivides `round(amount × 5)` times (`SMOOTH_LEVELS`, fewer past 400 000 triangles). Off: interpolating eight-point butterfly (a missing wing falls back to the midpoint). On: Loop (approximating). The Taubin pass and the 40° sharp-edge lock are gone. Points on open or non-manifold edges stay put; the bottom keeps its height so models stay on the floor. Colours and textures follow their faces as before.
- Smooth edges starts off. `--tool smooth-0.5-edges` turns it on for screenshots.
- A first try, averaging without subdivision, crumpled a cylinder (its cap fans pull points across the shape), and was dropped.
- Follow-up: on the app's own cylinder (caps fanned from one rim point) Smooth pinched a vertical crease from that point down the side, as if the object had a slit. `solid::center_fans` now rebuilds a flat, single-colour, untextured disc patch with a point on more than eight of its faces as a fan from its centre before subdividing (only when every new face keeps its direction). A first try that flipped edges to even out the valences left long slivers across the caps and spiky results, and was dropped.
- Follow-up: the camel scan (1.39 M triangles) was refused ("Too many triangles to smooth"), while the original smooths it at half and it looks the same afterwards. The slider now asks for a triangle size, `0.286 × diagonal × 16^-amount` (fitted to the original's cube: 3 splits at half, 5 at full), and `solid::smooth_levels` splits as often as it takes the median edge to get there, fewer past the 400 000-triangle budget. A model already finer than that (any scan) is left as it is; nothing is refused for its size any more.

## Smooth from the original's code

- The original's `Builder3D.exe` was disassembled (`PrintPreview::CSmoothMesh`; addresses in the session notes). The slider is an integer pass count (`SetIterations`, default 3, up to 6), not a triangle size. Smooth edges on: one Loop subdivision step per pass (edge 3/8·(a+b) + 1/8·(c+d), β = 3/(8n), 3/16 for n ≤ 3), no limit. Off: while the mesh has fewer than 4096 points, a plain midpoint 1→4 split; then 10 rounds of HC Laplacian smoothing (α 0.2, β 0.8), edges weighted by the mean opposite angle in radians, angles under 0.03 or over 1.5408 counting as 0. No crease handling, no remeshing.
- `solid::smooth_by` now does exactly that (`SMOOTH_STEPS` 6, `SPLIT_BELOW_POINTS` 4096, `hc_smooth`). The butterfly guess and the size-based levels are gone. Kept beyond the original: open/non-manifold edges stay put, flat fans are rebuilt from their centre, the bottom keeps its height, and Loop stops at 400 000 triangles (the original has no cap).
- This explains the captures: a cube at half and at full keeps its shape (the split stops at 4096 points, HC keeps the volume: a 40 mm cube becomes a 43 mm pillow), and the camel scan keeps its 1.39 M triangles while its mesh evens out. Smoothing the camel takes about 5 s at half, 9 s at full (release); the "Smoothing..." card now shows for models over 200 000 triangles.
- Smooth starts at 3 of 6 passes (the slider at half), as the original; the slider snaps to whole passes. `--tool smooth` keeps that default.
- `style::stepped_slider`: the knob jumps between the stops while dragged and the value changes only on release (or a click), so the work behind it runs once. Smooth uses it with its 6 passes.
- Split: the wireframe of the part the cut plane hides was still drawn. `fs_wire` now discards above the clip plane like the surface shader does.
